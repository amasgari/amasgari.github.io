## Abstract
For any positive integer $$r$$, the $$r$$-Fubini number with parameter $$n$$, denoted by $$F_{n,r}$$, is equal to the number of ways that the elements of a set with $$n+r$$ elements can be weakly ordered such that the $$r$$ least elements are in distinct orders. In this article we focus on the sequence of residues of the $$r$$-Fubini numbers modulo an arbitrary positive integer $$s$$ and show that this sequence is periodic and then, exhibit how to calculate its period length.
