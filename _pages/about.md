I am a PhD Student at the Cheriton School of Computer Science, **University of Waterloo**, advised by [Éric Schost](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~eschost/). Previously, I obtained my master's degree at the **University of Calgary** under an interdisciplinary program affiliated with the Departments of Computer Science and Mathematics, advised by [Michael Jacobson](https://pages.cpsc.ucalgary.ca/~jacobs/) and [Renate Scheidler](https://pages.cpsc.ucalgary.ca/~rscheidl/). Before that, I finished my bachelor's in Iran at the Department of Computer Engineering, **Sharif University of Technology**.

## Research
I am going to pursue research in the broad areas of **Computer Algebra** and **Symbolic Computation** during my PhD. My master's research mainly falls under **Computational and Algorithmic Number Theory**, and it involved developing software to realize faster Jacobian arithmetic on hyperelliptic curves (the thesis is available [here](https://ucalgary.scholaris.ca/items/8a1362fa-7cc8-4420-a8ea-9f8af0d8efcd)).

During my undergraduates, I also had some research and industry experience in Data Science when I worked on addressing the biased data problem for recommendation systems under a bachelor's thesis (in Persian), along with a summer internship at [Tapsell](https://tapsell.com/en/).

And I was fortunate to be first exposed to serious mathematical research as a student at Allameh Helli High School of Tehran taking a math research course with Majid Jahangiri, which led to [my first publication](/publication/2018-05-08-On-the-periodicity-problem-for-residual-r-Fubini-sequences), before starting my bachelor's.
